Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 November 2026.

Potato Merge Uploader ("the app") is a personal desktop tool used only by its owner to send their own videos to their own TikTok account as drafts.

Data the app receives from TikTok

  • Basic profile information (user.info.basic): open ID, display name and avatar of the authorizing account.
  • An access token and a refresh token, used only to upload drafts (video.upload).

How it is used and stored

Tokens are stored in a local file on the owner's computer and are used only to call the TikTok Content Posting API. Nothing is sent to any other server. No data is sold, shared, or used for advertising or analytics.

Retention and deletion

Deleting the local token file, or removing the app's access in the TikTok app settings, deletes or revokes all data the app holds.

Changes

This policy may change. The date above shows the latest version.
